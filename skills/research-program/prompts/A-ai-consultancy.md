# AI Consultancy Market Study — Services, Engagement Models, and Pricing (Global, Regional, Hungary) — Landscape Study

## Scope discipline
⚠️ High-level market/landscape research ONLY. Deliver service taxonomies, engagement models, and pricing structures with evidence.
🚫 No vendor "pick X" verdicts, no implementation plans, no sales pitches, no effort estimates, no timelines, no contract templates. Audience: decision-makers evaluating how to position or buy AI consultancy.

## Context
📌 No local baseline files; this is an external market study. Frame: 2025–2026 AI consulting market, from global system integrators to boutique specialists.

## Primary question
🔬 How do AI consultancy providers (global, EU/regional, and Hungarian) structure their service portfolios, engagement/delivery models, and pricing in 2025–2026, and what patterns define each market tier?

## Sub-questions (cover all)
🧭 A) Provider landscape & taxonomy: global SIs/consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey/QuantumBlack, BCG X, IBM Consulting, Infosys, TCS, Capgemini, Slalom); AI-native boutiques and advisory firms (e.g., Neudesic, Fractal, QuantumBlack, Harnham); cloud-partner ecosystems (AWS ProServe, Google Cloud Consulting, Microsoft AI partners); Hungarian players (regional offices of globals, local firms e.g. NNG-affiliated consultancies, Liferay/EDS-style local SIs, Hungarian boutiques and ML shops).
B) Service offerings: strategy/advisory, GenAI readiness, data & platform builds, ML/MLOps delivery, AI governance/compliance (EU AI Act), change management/adoption, managed services.
C) Engagement models: time & materials, fixed-scope projects, retainers, outcome/value-based pricing, dedicated pods, fractional AI leadership, enablement/co-build with client teams.
D) Pricing: rate cards/ranges by region and seniority, project-size bands, retainer structures, value-sharing evidence; EU AI Act compliance as priced workstream.
E) Demand & market dynamics: buyer segments (enterprise vs. mid-market vs. public sector), CEE/Hungary-specific factors (nearshoring, EU funding, local talent rates), 2024–2026 market growth and consolidation, notable wins and failed/rolled-back engagements.

## Sources
📚 ≥15 sources, 3+ independent per major claim. Prefer consulting-firm market reports read critically, analyst reports (Gartner, IDC, Everest, Forrester), procurement/RFP disclosures, Hungarian/EU market data (GVI, KSH, Eurostat, EU AI Act texts), rate surveys, practitioner accounts (LinkedIn essays, conference talks), Hungarian firm websites and case studies. Mandatory credibility scoring and triangulation.

## Critique personas
🧑‍⚖️ Skeptical Practitioner · Adversarial Compliance Reviewer (EU AI Act, pricing-transparency lens) · Buyer/Procurement Lens · CFO Lens (cost realism of rate cards) · Hungarian Market Insider.

## Deliverable (English, analytical register)
📄 1) Executive Summary 300–400 words — directional stance on how each market tier differs. 2) Findings 1800–2500 words, 80%+ prose: provider taxonomy · service portfolio map · engagement-model comparison · pricing structures with concrete ranges where evidenced · Hungary/regional deep dive. 3) Qualitative comparison matrix: tiers × (service breadth, delivery model, pricing model, rate level, compliance offer, proximity). 4) Directional observations: where the market is moving (productized services, outcome pricing, AI Act-driven demand). 5) Open questions. 6) Full bibliography with credibility tags.
